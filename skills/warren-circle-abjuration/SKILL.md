name: warren-circle-abjuration
description: Check risks, permissions, and acceptance criteria as Abjuration. Use it for a security, governance, or independent review Quest.
---

1. Start with the agreed boundaries and review criteria. Identify the risks, permissions, or deliverables the Quest asks you to check.
2. Review the evidence independently when the Quest calls for it. Record the checks you ran, their results, and any unresolved risk. Ask Lucerna if a check needs permission the Covenant does not give.
3. Return the requested risk register, test evidence, or review findings. Tie each finding to the relevant evidence and criteria.

## Runes

Choose the proposed Runes that fit the Quest. Use Relics only with Covenant authorization.

- **Threat** (`abjuration.threat`). Lists approved assets, plausible risks, and safeguards that warrant review.
- **Boundary** (`abjuration.boundary`). Maps where data or requests cross trust and access boundaries.
- **Privilege** (`abjuration.privilege`). Compares granted access with task needs and flags unnecessary permissions.
- **Assessment** (`abjuration.assessment`). Reviews approved controls and records the evidence and gaps for each one.
- **Risk** (`abjuration.risk`). Ranks identified risks by likelihood and impact with reasons for the order.
- **Privacy** (`abjuration.privacy`). Flags where sensitive data may be exposed and what needs review.
- **Remediation** (`abjuration.remediation`). Recommends fixes for an identified risk and explains the expected improvement.
- **Review** (`abjuration.review`). Checks whether the evidence shows that a proposed safeguard is working.
- **Escalation** (`abjuration.escalation`). Summarizes unresolved risks, likely impact, and the decision a person needs to make.
- **Criteria** (`abjuration.criteria`). Turns a requirement into observable conditions a reviewer can check.
- **Plan** (`abjuration.plan`). Lists test cases, expected outcomes, and important gaps in coverage.
- **Regression** (`abjuration.regression`). Checks previously working behavior and reports any changes after an update.
- **Acceptance** (`abjuration.acceptance`). Checks a deliverable against its agreed criteria and records the evidence.
- **Edge** (`abjuration.edge`). Checks valid boundaries and failure conditions and records unexpected behavior.
- **Reproduce** (`abjuration.reproduce`). Writes the conditions and steps needed to observe a reported defect.
- **Evidence** (`abjuration.evidence`). Records the actual result, test conditions, and supporting evidence.
- **Triage** (`abjuration.triage`). Ranks defects by user impact and identifies which need attention first.
- **Report** (`abjuration.report`). Summarizes test coverage, passes, failures, and remaining limits.
