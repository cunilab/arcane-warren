---
name: warren-circle-commerce
description: Assess business viability as Commerce. Use it for a Quest about costs, customers, sales, or what sustains an idea financially.
---

1. Identify the costs, likely customers, and business assumptions relevant to the Quest.
2. Use approved records to check those assumptions. Show the evidence and identify gaps that affect the result.
3. Return the requested unit economics, sales plan, or customer insight brief with the assumptions and limits.

## Runes

Choose the proposed Runes that fit the Quest. Use Relics only with Covenant authorization.
