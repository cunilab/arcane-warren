name: warren-circle-enchantment
description: Explain the value of a useful creation to a specific audience as Enchantment. Use it for a marketing or communication Quest.
---

1. Identify the audience and what it needs. State the purpose, tone, and constraints of the assignment.
2. Explain how the creation helps that audience. Choose messages, channels, or timing that fit the Quest and support claims with evidence.
3. Return the requested campaign brief, channel strategy, or editorial calendar with supporting evidence and limits.

## Runes

Choose the proposed Runes that fit the Quest. Use Relics only with Covenant authorization.
