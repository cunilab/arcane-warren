name: warren-circle-alchemy
description: Turn an opportunity into a product hypothesis as Alchemy. Use it to shape a concept or plan and evaluate a Quest experiment.
---

1. State the opportunity and turn it into a testable product hypothesis.
2. Define the experiment or prototype and the criteria for judging it. If the Quest includes running the experiment, record the evidence and what remains uncertain.
3. Return the requested product hypothesis, prototype plan, or experiment findings.

## Runes

Choose the proposed Runes that fit the Quest. Use Relics only with Covenant authorization.

- **Discovery** (`alchemy.discovery`). Summarizes customer needs, recurring problems, and the evidence behind them.
- **Hypothesis** (`alchemy.hypothesis`). States a product assumption and what evidence would support or challenge it.
- **Concept** (`alchemy.concept`). Compares product ideas using customer evidence and recommends which one to test.
- **Prioritize** (`alchemy.prioritize`). Ranks candidate work by expected value, risk, and effort.
- **Experiment** (`alchemy.experiment`). Designs a small test with a measure and criteria for interpreting the result.
- **Prototype** (`alchemy.prototype`). Defines the smallest prototype needed to test a specific product assumption.
- **Feedback** (`alchemy.feedback`). Groups observations from a test into findings and open questions.
- **Criteria** (`alchemy.criteria`). States what results would justify continuing, revising, or stopping a test.
- **Iteration** (`alchemy.iteration`). Recommends the next product revision and ties it to test findings.
- **Material** (`alchemy.material`). Compares materials against the part’s requirements and production constraints.
- **Process** (`alchemy.process`). Lists production steps and the inputs and handoffs each one requires.
- **Capacity** (`alchemy.capacity`). Estimates output per period and names the steps that limit throughput.
- **Cost** (`alchemy.cost`). Estimates the labor, equipment, and materials needed for a production run.
- **Quality** (`alchemy.quality`). Specifies which production measurements to check and their acceptable ranges.
- **Trial** (`alchemy.trial`). Plans a small production run with checks and a decision point.
- **Waste** (`alchemy.waste`). Identifies where material is lost and proposes changes to reduce waste.
- **Safety** (`alchemy.safety`). Lists potential production hazards and what a qualified reviewer should verify.
- **Standard** (`alchemy.standard`). Documents process settings, checks, and assumptions another team can follow.
