---
name: warren-circle-divination
description: Gather and check evidence as Divination. Use it when a research or intelligence Quest needs to show what is known and what remains uncertain.
---

1. Turn the decision into an answerable question. State what evidence would answer it.
2. Gather and verify relevant sources within the Quest scope. Record where the evidence came from and identify gaps or disagreements.
3. Return the requested evidence brief, analysis report, or uncertainty assessment. Make it clear which findings the available evidence supports.

## Runes

Choose the proposed Runes that fit the Quest. Use Relics only with Covenant authorization.

- **Question** (`divination.question`). Writes an answerable research question with scope and evidence criteria.
- **Discovery** (`divination.discovery`). Finds primary sources and notes how each relates to the research question.
- **Verification** (`divination.verification`). Checks key claims against independent evidence and records agreement or conflict.
- **Provenance** (`divination.provenance`). Records source links, dates, and origins so findings can be checked.
- **Synthesis** (`divination.synthesis`). Combines evidence into a conclusion and explains where sources disagree.
- **Uncertainty** (`divination.confidence-grading`). Summarizes how reliable a finding is and what evidence is still missing.
- **Comparison** (`divination.comparison`). Compares plausible explanations against the same evidence and lists unresolved points.
- **Brief** (`divination.evidence-brief`). Produces a short decision brief with findings, sources, and uncertainty.
- **Update** (`divination.update`). Identifies conclusions that depend on old evidence and names what to refresh.
- **Intake** (`divination.intake`). Summarizes fields, record counts, data sources, and obvious quality problems.
- **Cleansing** (`divination.cleansing`). Standardizes inconsistent values and records how missing data was handled.
- **Measure** (`divination.measure`). Defines each metric’s calculation, denominator, and units before comparison.
- **Explore** (`divination.explore`). Surfaces notable data patterns and flags anomalies for follow-up.
- **Compare** (`divination.compare`). Measures differences between groups against a stated reference or baseline.
- **Uncertainty** (`divination.variation-assessment`). Reports the variation, sample limits, or assumptions behind a result.
- **Visualize** (`divination.visualize`). Turns data into a chart with clear measures, labels, and units.
- **Validate** (`divination.validate`). Rechecks formulas and assumptions and flags results that need correction.
- **Reproduce** (`divination.reproduce`). Records the inputs, transformations, and steps needed to rerun an analysis.
- **Scan** (`divination.scan`). Summarizes recent market signals and separates observations from open questions.
- **Map** (`divination.map`). Lists competitors and compares the offers they make to customers.
- **Segment** (`divination.segment`). Groups potential customers by needs and explains what distinguishes each group.
- **Demand** (`divination.demand`). Summarizes evidence of interest and explains the limits of each signal.
- **Trend** (`divination.trend`). Places a market pattern in context and notes whether it appears to be growing.
- **Gap** (`divination.gap`). Identifies underserved needs and explains what makes them difficult to address.
- **Benchmark** (`divination.benchmark`). Compares relevant prices and features across a consistent set of alternatives.
- **Prioritize** (`divination.prioritize`). Ranks market hypotheses by supporting evidence and identifies what to test next.
- **Brief** (`divination.opportunity-map`). Produces an opportunity map with customer needs, alternatives, and evidence gaps.
