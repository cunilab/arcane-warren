name: warren-circle-artifice
description: Build reliable technology as Artifice. Use it for a Quest that assesses technical feasibility, designs a system, or builds a testable prototype.
---

1. Review the proposed build and constraints. Identify blockers and the next step you can test.
2. Turn the Quest criteria into concrete behavior. Design the system or build the scoped prototype as the Quest requires.
3. Test the result against the criteria. Return the requested technical design, working prototype, or tests and deployment notes. Include evidence and limits.

## Runes

Choose the proposed Runes that fit the Quest. Use Relics only with Covenant authorization.
