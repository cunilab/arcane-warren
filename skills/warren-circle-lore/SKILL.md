---
name: warren-circle-lore
description: Turn notes, decisions, and outcomes into reusable knowledge as Lore. Use it for a Quest that needs a decision record, knowledge brief, or memory review.
---

1. Review the authorized records supplied with the Quest. Check the source and scope of each relevant claim.
2. Organize the decisions and lessons so a future Quest can find and use them. Record the evidence, context, and limits.
3. Return the requested knowledge brief, decision record, or memory review to Lucerna, with any gaps or proposed corrections.

## Runes

Choose the proposed Runes that fit the Quest. Use Relics only with Covenant authorization.

- **Map** (`lore.map`). Connects related records by topic and makes their relationships visible.
- **Curate** (`lore.curate`). Selects durable lessons and keeps the evidence and context needed to reuse them.
- **Provenance** (`lore.provenance`). Records where a finding came from, when it was made, and why it matters.
- **Scope** (`lore.scope`). Identifies who should have access to a record and where it belongs.
- **Retrieve** (`lore.retrieve`). Ranks retrieved records by relevance and explains which ones support the task.
- **Deduplicate** (`lore.deduplicate`). Groups overlapping records and identifies the clearest version to keep.
- **Correct** (`lore.correct`). Flags conflicts or stale claims and names the evidence needed to resolve them.
- **Taxonomy** (`lore.taxonomy`). Updates categories so similar records can be filed and found consistently.
- **Review** (`lore.review`). Checks records for accuracy, usefulness, and reasons to retain them.
- **Capture** (`lore.capture`). Records what was decided, what happened, and why it matters later.
- **Structure** (`lore.structure`). Organizes headings and sections around what readers need to find.
- **Technical** (`lore.technical`). Explains inputs, outputs, and usage with concrete examples.
- **Decision** (`lore.decision`). Writes a record of the choice, alternatives, and trade-offs.
- **Process** (`lore.process`). Writes instructions with prerequisites, ordered steps, and completion checks.
- **Edit** (`lore.edit`). Shortens and clarifies a draft while keeping its essential meaning.
- **Version** (`lore.version`). Records changes and marks which revision readers should use.
- **Verify** (`lore.verify`). Checks claims against sources and flags statements that need evidence.
- **Handoff** (`lore.handoff`). Packages decisions, instructions, and open questions for the next reader.
- **Synthesis** (`lore.synthesis`). Combines relevant specialist findings and explains where they agree or differ.
- **Critique** (`lore.critique`). Identifies unsupported assumptions and explains what would strengthen the argument.
- **Trade-off** (`lore.trade-off`). Compares options using the same criteria and makes their costs visible.
- **Perspective** (`lore.perspective`). Summarizes each position’s strongest reasoning and central disagreement.
- **Uncertainty** (`lore.uncertainty`). Names missing evidence and explains how it could change a decision.
- **Scenario** (`lore.scenario`). Describes plausible outcomes under different assumptions and notes what would distinguish them.
- **Prioritize** (`lore.prioritize`). Ranks decision criteria and explains which ones matter most in context.
- **Mentor** (`lore.mentor`). Suggests questions or exercises that help someone improve their reasoning.
- **Advise** (`lore.advise`). Recommends a course of action and explains the evidence and trade-offs behind it.
