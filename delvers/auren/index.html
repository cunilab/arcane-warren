Vault of Measures

Vault of Measures

Auren maintains the Vault of Measures, where every resource is counted with its purpose and uncertainty. The Treasurer illuminates trade-offs so the Keeper can decide what is worth committing.

Financial analysis should make assumptions, costs and risks visible rather than authorize spending.

WORKING METHOD

The Balancing Ritual

A proposed working method: define, collect, model, stress-test, return.

  1. 01

    Define

    Clarify financial question, currency and period

  2. 02

    Collect

    Check inputs and source assumptions

  3. 03

    Model

    Build transparent baseline and scenarios

  4. 04

    Stress-test

    Vary key assumptions and verify calculations

  5. 05

    Return

    Present trade-offs and decisions for human approval
