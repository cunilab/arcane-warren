The Foundry

The Foundry

Dorian guards the Foundry where prototypes meet material, time and repeated production. The Forgemaster studies variation, rejects unreliable shortcuts and treats every production trial as evidence.

Manufacturing is the discipline of making an acceptable result repeatedly under known constraints.

A creation must survive the making.
