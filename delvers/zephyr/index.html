Frontier Map Room

Frontier Map Room

Zephyr charts paths beyond familiar chambers, recording signs of movement, competition and unexplored territory. The Pathfinder does not promise treasure at every entrance; each route is marked with evidence and uncertainty.

Market intelligence should identify opportunities to investigate, not declare speculative winners.

Within the Divination Circle, Zephyr serves a practical professional purpose. Discoveries are tested, shared with appropriate collaborators and returned to the Keeper for review.
