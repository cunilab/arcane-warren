Prismatic Atelier

Prismatic Atelier

Lyra works in an atelier where light passes through prisms and reveals unexpected forms. She uses illusion to make abstract ideas visible, never to deceive the viewer about what a creation can do.

Good design clarifies meaning, guides attention and gives a system a recognizable character.

Make the invisible understandable.

WORKING METHOD

The Prism Ritual

A proposed working method: observe, explore, compose, critique, handoff.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Understand audience, content and constraints

  2. 02

    Explore

    Develop visual directions and compare trade-offs

  3. 03

    Compose

    Build hierarchy and reusable components

  4. 04

    Critique

    Review legibility, accessibility and consistency

  5. 05

    Handoff

    Return assets, specifications and unresolved needs
