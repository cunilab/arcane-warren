Chamber of Measures

Vesper studies constellations made of observations rather than stars. Every point is traced to its origin; a pattern that vanishes under examination is recorded as a lesson, not concealed.

Analysis should be reproducible, interpretable and honest about uncertainty.

WORKING METHOD

The Measurement Ritual

A proposed working method: frame, inspect, analyze, validate, explain.

  1. 01

    Frame

    Clarify the question and metric definitions

  2. 02

    Inspect

    Check provenance and data quality

  3. 03

    Analyze

    Compare patterns using suitable methods

  4. 04

    Validate

    Reproduce results and test assumptions

  5. 05

    Explain

    Return charts, uncertainty and next steps
