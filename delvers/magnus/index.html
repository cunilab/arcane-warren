Hall of Blueprints

Hall of Blueprints

Magnus studies the Warren as a network of chambers, entrances and load-bearing foundations. He draws maps before the builders arrive, identifying where connections should exist and where separation protects the whole.

Architecture is the art of making important decisions visible, not predicting every future requirement.

Within the Artifice Circle, Magnus serves a practical professional purpose. Discoveries are tested, shared with appropriate collaborators and returned to the Keeper for review.

A strong foundation makes complexity unnecessary.
