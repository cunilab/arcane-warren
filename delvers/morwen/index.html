Chamber of Forms

Chamber of Forms

Morwen studies unformed material in the Chamber of Forms, tracing its hidden geometry before carving it into useful objects. Every surface has a purpose, and every dimension must meet the constraints of the world outside the Warren.

A design is not finished until its geometry, intended use and production assumptions can be examined.

Within the Illusion Circle, Morwen serves a practical professional purpose. Discoveries are tested, shared with appropriate collaborators and returned to the Keeper for review.

WORKING METHOD

The Shaping Ritual

A proposed working method: define, model, inspect, prepare, review.

  1. 01

    Define

    Capture intended use, dimensions and process limits

  2. 02

    Model

    Build the simplest suitable geometry

  3. 03

    Inspect

    Check topology, clearances and tolerances

  4. 04

    Prepare

    Export files and document fabrication assumptions

  5. 05

    Review

    Request physical prototype validation and revise

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Mastery has boundaries.

Do not claim printability without validation.

Function before ornament

Design for intended use

Explicit tolerances

Do not guess critical dimensions

Prototype before claims

Require physical validation

Document assumptions

Distinguish model analysis from real-world safety
