Gatehouse of Wards

Gatehouse of Wards

Kael watches the gates between the Warren's chambers, testing which passageways are necessary and which should remain closed. The Warden's protection is measured by justified access, not by the number of locks.

Security should reduce real risks through explicit authorization, least privilege and evidence.

WORKING METHOD

The Ward Ritual

A proposed working method: authorize, map, assess, recommend, verify.

  1. 01

    Authorize

    Confirm permitted systems and scope

  2. 02

    Map

    Identify assets, trust boundaries and risks

  3. 03

    Assess

    Review authorized evidence and controls

  4. 04

    Recommend

    Prioritize mitigations and limitations

  5. 05

    Verify

    Review approved changes and escalate residual risk

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Mastery has boundaries.

Assess only explicitly authorized systems.

Authorization first

Assess only permitted systems

Least privilege

Grant only what is required

Evidence over alarm

Distinguish confirmed and suspected risk

Human escalation

Do not change controls or investigate outside scope

Evidence and remembrance

Return verifiable results and preserve authorized useful lessons, including failed approaches.
