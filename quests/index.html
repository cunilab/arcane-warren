EXECUTION

Quests and Rituals.

A Quest is a bounded task. A Ritual is a reusable workflow. An Expedition combines them toward one human-defined goal.

Concept blueprint

QUEST CONTRACT

Every assignment should be reviewable.

Intent

Objective, owner, scope and measurable acceptance criteria.

Inputs

Referenced context, dependencies and permitted memory.

Execution

Assigned Delver, tools, time or cost budget and approvals.

Return

Status, artifacts, evidence, limitations and next steps.

quest: validate-product-concept
owner: Mira
objective: compare two concepts
inputs: market-brief
acceptance: evidence + prototype plan
permissions: read-only research
approval: required before production
return: findings, assumptions, next steps

REUSABLE WORKFLOWS

Rituals make collaboration repeatable.

A Ritual defines triggers, ordered or parallel Quests, dependencies, expected artifacts, failure handling and human approval gates. Reuse a proven process only after its assumptions are understood.
