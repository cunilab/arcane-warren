GOVERNANCE

Autonomy has boundaries.

A Covenant defines a worker's permitted scope, tools, constraints and escalation requirements.

Concept blueprint

AUTHORIZATION

Least privilege by design.

Read and propose

Use only authorized information; prepare drafts and proposals within scope.

External action

Publishing, purchases, customer contact and production changes require explicit permission.

Escalation

Stop on ambiguous instructions, unavailable permissions, unsafe conditions or exhausted budgets.

Auditability

Record approval, evidence, action outcomes and failures in the Chronicle.

APPROVAL EXAMPLES

Authority follows consequences.

ActionProposed gate
Draft a campaignWithin scoped Quest
Publish a campaignHuman approval
Change production infrastructureHuman approval and rollback plan
Spend fundsExplicit approved budget and action
Retrieve personal or project memoryVerified identity and authorized scope

Policy enforcement and approval interfaces are design requirements, not implemented capabilities.
