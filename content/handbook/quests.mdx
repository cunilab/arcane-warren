## Quest contract

Use this blank contract to write a Quest. Fill in each field before handing it to a Circle.

<dl className="not-prose warren-brief warren-contract" aria-label="Blank Quest contract">
  <div><dt>Intent</dt><dd>Objective, Circle owner, scope, and acceptance criteria.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Inputs</dt><dd>Source references, dependency outputs, and authorized memory.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Execution</dt><dd>Allowed tools and actions, time or cost limit, and approval points.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Return</dt><dd>Deliverable, status, evidence, limitations, and decisions for review.</dd></div>
</dl>

## When Quests work together

An [Expedition](/architecture/#an-expedition-from-start-to-finish) groups Quests around a shared goal. A **Ritual** defines a repeatable workflow.

<PlaceholderImage id="diagrams/ritual-handoffs" />

- **Start** with a trigger and its required inputs.
- **Assign** owners and state which Quests run in order or in parallel.
- **Hand off** expected artifacts and the context the next Circle may use.
- **Handle failures** with explicit stop, retry, and escalation rules.
- **Review** the evidence and record any required human approval.
