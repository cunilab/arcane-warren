<RoleHero id="characters/keeper" title="The Keeper" description="The person who sets direction, approves consequential actions, and decides when the work is complete.">
  <p>The Keeper is the human owner of an Expedition. They decide what problem to solve, what Circles may do, and which results justify a decision.</p>
</RoleHero>

## Before work begins

Give Lucerna an objective, scope, time or cost limit, and a result you can review. Specify the data and tools the work may use, the criteria for accepting the result, and when work should pause for a decision.

## Decisions the Keeper owns

<dl className="not-prose warren-card-grid" aria-label="Keeper decisions">
  <div className="warren-panel"><dt>Change the scope</dt><dd>Review what changed, why it matters, and how it affects the agreed result.</dd></div>
  <div className="warren-panel"><dt>Approve an action</dt><dd>Review the specific action, its consequences, and the available safeguards.</dd></div>
  <div className="warren-panel"><dt>Accept a result</dt><dd>Check the deliverable, evidence against the criteria, and unresolved risks.</dd></div>
  <div className="warren-panel"><dt>Pause or stop</dt><dd>Decide which work stops, what to preserve, and what still needs follow-up.</dd></div>
</dl>

The Keeper can request independent review for disputed or consequential results. Lucerna brings the evidence and options. The Keeper makes the decision.

## After review

Accept the result, request a revision, or close the work with the reason recorded. Review proposed changes to [Circle instructions](/grimoire/) or [shared lessons](/memory/). Record approvals and their scope in the Chronicle.
