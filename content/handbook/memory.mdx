[Cairn Memory](https://github.com/cunilab/cairn-memory) is the independent project selected for the Warren’s planned memory integration. Warren owns Quest artifacts and the Chronicle.

## What gets recorded

<div className="warren-table" role="region" aria-label="Warren work and memory records" tabIndex={0}>

| Record | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Quest artifact** | The delivered brief, design, patch, or other result, with evidence a reviewer can check. |
| **Chronicle** | The execution history, including assignments, state changes, approvals, and failures. |
| **Cairn lesson** | A reusable finding or decision with evidence, context, and limits on where it applies. |

</div>

## Recall, check, record

<PlaceholderImage id="diagrams/memory-loop" />

<ol className="not-prose warren-diagram-key" aria-label="Memory and lessons lifecycle">
  <li><strong>Recall</strong><p>Retrieve authorized records relevant to the Quest.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Explore</strong><p>Check the records against current evidence and scope.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Distill</strong><p>Capture what was learned, including its evidence, provenance, and context.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Preserve</strong><p>Save reusable lessons and correct records when better evidence changes the finding.</p></li>
</ol>
