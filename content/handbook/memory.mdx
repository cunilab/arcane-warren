[Cairn Memory](https://github.com/cunilab/cairn-memory) is the independent memory service chosen for the Warren. Quest artifacts stay with their Quests. Lucerna stores the Chronicle and model notes in Cairn.

## What gets recorded

<div className="warren-table" role="region" aria-label="Warren work and memory records" tabIndex={0}>

| Record | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Quest artifact** | The delivered brief, design, patch, or other result, with evidence a reviewer can check. |
| **Chronicle** | One Cairn note per completed, failed, or canceled Quest. It records the Circle, model choices, state changes, approvals, and outcome. |
| **Model capabilities** | What previously chosen models can do, such as edit files or accept images. |
| **Usage-limit notes** | Dated records of when a model hit a limit, shown to the Keeper as hints. |
| **Cairn lesson** | A reusable finding or decision with evidence, context, and limits on where it applies. |

</div>

See the [Lucerna skill](/skills/warren-lucerna/) for when these notes are saved.

## Recall, check, record

<PlaceholderImage id="diagrams/memory-loop" />

<ol className="not-prose warren-diagram-key" aria-label="Memory and lessons lifecycle">
  <li><strong>Recall</strong><p>Retrieve authorized records relevant to the Quest.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Explore</strong><p>Check the records against current evidence and scope.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Distill</strong><p>Capture what was learned, including its evidence, provenance, and context.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Preserve</strong><p>Save reusable lessons and correct records when better evidence changes the finding.</p></li>
</ol>

Before this is connected to a live system, check who is asking, what they are allowed to see, how records are searched and saved, how corrections and deletions work, and how long records are kept. If Cairn is unavailable, Lucerna tells the Keeper once and continues.
