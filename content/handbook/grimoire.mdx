A **Grimoire** describes how a Circle should work across assignments. Its **Spellbook** is its collection of [Runes](/runes/).

## What an instruction profile contains

<PlaceholderImage id="diagrams/circle-anatomy" />

<dl className="not-prose warren-diagram-key" aria-label="Circle instruction, Quest, and authority fields">
  <div><dt>Grimoire</dt><dd>Circle identity, responsibilities, working method, evidence standards, and escalation.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Quest</dt><dd>Current objective, inputs, expected output, and acceptance criteria.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Covenant</dt><dd>Allowed data, tools, actions, and approvals. It governs what the Circle may do.</dd></div>
</dl>

The [Rune catalog](/runes/) records each skill’s fixed name, its version, what it takes in, what it returns, how the result is judged, and which tools it may use. The [Covenant](/covenants/) governs access to tools and data for the current [Quest](/quests/).

## Change instructions with evidence

1. Record which instruction helped or failed and attach the evidence.
2. Have the Keeper review the change and version the approved profile.
3. Preserve reusable lessons in [Cairn](/memory/) within the intended access scope.
