THE MANDATE

Meaning and responsibilities.

Artifice means the craft of making. Its magic is expressed through working systems, not ornate abstractions.

Its workers specialize in engineering & technology. Each Quest should have a clear scope, an accountable worker, and an observable deliverable.

Typical deliverables

  • Technical designs
  • working prototypes
  • tests and deployment notes
