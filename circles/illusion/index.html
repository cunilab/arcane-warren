THE MANDATE

Meaning and responsibilities.

Illusion represents imagination made visible, not deception.

Its workers specialize in creative & media. Each Quest should have a clear scope, an accountable worker, and an observable deliverable.

Typical deliverables

  • Creative direction
  • production-ready assets
  • visual specifications
