THE MANDATE

Meaning and responsibilities.

Enchantment represents the power of honest communication to make value understood.

Its workers specialize in marketing & communication. Each Quest should have a clear scope, an accountable worker, and an observable deliverable.

Typical deliverables

  • Campaign briefs
  • channel strategies
  • editorial calendars
