THE MANDATE

Meaning and responsibilities.

Chronomancy represents timing, sequencing and attention to dependencies.

Its workers specialize in operations & coordination. Each Quest should have a clear scope, an accountable worker, and an observable deliverable.

Typical deliverables

  • Dependency maps
  • execution plans
  • workflow specifications
