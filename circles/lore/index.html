THE MANDATE

Meaning and responsibilities.

Lore represents understanding accumulated across journeys.

Its workers specialize in knowledge & learning. Each Quest should have a clear scope, an accountable worker, and an observable deliverable.

Typical deliverables

  • Knowledge briefs
  • decision records
  • memory reviews
