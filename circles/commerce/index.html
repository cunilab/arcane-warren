THE MANDATE

Meaning and responsibilities.

Commerce represents exchange and responsible stewardship of resources.

Its workers specialize in business & finance. Each Quest should have a clear scope, an accountable worker, and an observable deliverable.

Typical deliverables

  • Unit economics
  • sales plans
  • customer insight briefs
