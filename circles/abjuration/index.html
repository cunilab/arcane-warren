THE MANDATE

Meaning and responsibilities.

Abjuration symbolizes protective boundaries and independent review.

Its workers specialize in security & governance. Each Quest should have a clear scope, an accountable worker, and an observable deliverable.

Typical deliverables

  • Risk registers
  • test evidence
  • review findings
